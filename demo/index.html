Hand Tracking Cursor

Control this page
without touching anything.

Turn on the camera in the trackpad at the bottom left. Move your hand to move the cursor. Touch your index finger to your thumb to click, and hold the pinch while moving up or down to scroll.

Jump to gestures

Gestures

Move

An open hand moves the arrow. The cursor leans into the direction it is travelling, then straightens back up when you hold still.

Tap

Bring your index finger to your thumb. The skeleton turns green, the arrow scales down, and the element underneath receives a real click.

Scroll

Hold the pinch and drag up or down. Whatever is under the cursor scrolls — the page, or a single panel — and it keeps gliding when you let go.

Scroll inside an element

Pinch over this list and drag. The list scrolls on its own; the page stays put.

Ordinary controls

Everything here responds to a pinch exactly as it does to a click.
